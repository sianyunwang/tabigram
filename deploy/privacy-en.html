Privacy Policy

This is a translation for convenience. If it differs from the Traditional Chinese version, the Chinese version prevails.

Key points

  • By default, your trip data is stored on your own device; it is backed up to the cloud only after you sign in.
  • We show no ads, do not sell data, and do not use third-party tracking or analytics tools such as Google Analytics. To improve the Service we only record anonymous usage counts (for example, how many devices opened the app today or how many share cards were saved), with no account, email, device ID, IP address or location. You can turn this off in your settings.
  • Your private notes, photos, extra expenses and check-in locations are visible only to you and are not shared with your travel companions.
  • Your location is recorded only once, when you tap "I'm here". It is not tracked in the background, and you can clear it at any time.
  • "Use my location" in "Pick stations" only finds the nearest station on this device. Your location is not saved and is not sent to any server.
  • When you stamp an airport stamp, whether you are near the airport is checked on this device only. We record only the airport code and the time, never your coordinates.
  • Flight lookups send only the flight number, date and airport code, never your identity or location.
  • TabigrAm.zine, TabigrAm.mise (our curated shop) and Trip Plans do not require signing in. Articles you save are kept only in your own browser.
  • The legal name that creators and writers enter when signing the creator license agreement is visible only to the operator of the Service and is never made public.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and all your cloud data at any time.

1. Who we are

TabigrAm (the "Service") is a personally run web app for planning travel itineraries, available at tabigram.info. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the operator of the Service. This policy explains how the Service handles your personal data. For the rules on using the Service, see the Terms of Service.

Travel docs, Travel Tape and Wishlist

  • Travel docs (boarding passes, QR codes, visas, bookings and other files you add): stored only in this device's browser as original files. They are never uploaded to our servers and never included in share cards, shared trips or exports. They disappear if you clear browser data or switch phones.
  • Travel Tape (a 30-second voice recording you make for yourself at the airport, plus the cover photo and title you choose for the case): requires signing in and is stored in a private cloud space, only you can see and hear it. It can't be edited once sealed, but you can delete it. It is deleted along with your account. The microphone is used only while you are recording. Videos you save during playback are made on your device and never uploaded.
  • Wishlist: part of the trip, so it syncs with the trip and your travel companions can see it. When you enter only a place name, we send that name to OpenStreetMap to find its coordinates (never your identity or location).

3. Who can see your data

  • Travel companions you invite: people who join the same trip with the 6-character invite code can see that trip's shared itinerary, as well as members' emails and display names.
  • Your private content (private plans, notes, photos, extra expenses, check-in locations): visible only to you. Neither your travel companions nor administrators can see it.
  • The operator of the Service: has technical access to the database for maintenance purposes, but will not look at your content except to handle your requests or fix problems.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data to anyone.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which features are used and where things go wrong, the app sends anonymous events to our own database (Supabase), which only keeps the total count of each kind of event per day, for example:

  • How many devices opened the app today (grouped by phone or computer, and whether it was added to the home screen), and how many were new or returning.
  • How many times trips were created, plans were added, the station picker was used (by region), TRA/THSR timetables were checked, share cards were saved (by template) and stickers were used (by sticker pack).
  • How many sign-ups and sign-ins happened (Google or email), and how many sync failures and errors occurred.
  • TabigrAm.zine, TabigrAm.mise and Trip Plans: how many times pages were opened, articles or items were viewed, articles were saved, “Buy” was tapped and share cards were made (by article, item or plan ID, never by who you are).

These statistics do not include your account, email, device ID, IP address, location, trip contents, station names or photos, and cannot be used to identify you. Whether a device was already counted today, and the date it was first used, are stored only in your own browser and are never sent.

You can turn this off by unchecking “Help improve TabigrAm” in your settings; TabigrAm.zine, TabigrAm.mise and Trip Plans in the same browser stop counting too. It is also turned off automatically when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

5. Where data is stored and for how long

  • Your device: data is stored in your browser's local storage until you delete the trip or clear the browser's site data. So that you can open the app without an internet connection, your browser also caches the web pages and fonts themselves (this contains no personal data other than your trip content).
  • The cloud: data is stored on Supabase's servers until you delete the trip or your account. When the trip creator deletes a trip, the whole trip in the cloud and all members' journals are deleted with it; when a travel companion deletes a trip, they leave it, and only their own journal and photos are deleted.

6. Data security

  • The whole site uses encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • The cloud database has access controls: each person can read and write only their own data, and trip content is visible only to members.
  • Photos are stored in non-public storage and can be accessed only by the person who uploaded them.
  • Creators' emails and review notes are never public; visitors who are not signed in can read only published works.

No online service can guarantee 100% security, but we will do our best to protect your data.

7. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act of the Republic of China (Taiwan), you may at any time:

  • Request access to, or view, your personal data
  • Ask us to correct your data
  • Ask us to stop using your data, or to delete your account and all your cloud data

You can delete trips yourself in the app. When signed in, you can also delete your account yourself under "Profile Settings → Delete Account"; the trips, journals and photos you created in the cloud will be deleted along with it. You can also make a request using the contact details below, and we will handle it as soon as possible.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

This policy may be updated as features change. When it is updated, the date at the top of this page will be changed. If there are significant changes, we will also notify you in the app.
